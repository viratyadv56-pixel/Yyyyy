Frequently asked questions

Who does the AI agent call?

Only people who enquired with you. We check calling rules before outbound goes live.

How do payments work?

₹17,500 to start and ₹17,500 at go-live. The monthly fee begins after the pilot and is billed in advance.

Can I add English or CRM integrations?

Yes. New languages, CRM integrations and extra features are quoted separately.

What do you need from me?

Your property list and the phone number the agent will call from.

How does the 14-day pilot work?

In week 1 we load your properties, tune the Hindi script and test calls with you. In week 2 the agent goes live on real leads. Then we review the results together and you decide whether to continue.
